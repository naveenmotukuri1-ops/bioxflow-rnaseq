# Citations

If you use bioxflow/rnaseq, please cite the tools it runs.

* **Nextflow** — Di Tommaso P, et al. Nextflow enables reproducible computational workflows. *Nat Biotechnol.* 2017;35:316-319.
* **nf-core** (pipeline structure and conventions) — Ewels PA, et al. The nf-core framework for community-curated bioinformatics pipelines. *Nat Biotechnol.* 2020;38:276-278.
* **FastQC** — Andrews S. FastQC: a quality control tool for high throughput sequence data. https://www.bioinformatics.babraham.ac.uk/projects/fastqc/
* **fastp** — Chen S, et al. fastp: an ultra-fast all-in-one FASTQ preprocessor. *Bioinformatics.* 2018;34:i884-i890.
* **Salmon** — Patro R, et al. Salmon provides fast and bias-aware quantification of transcript expression. *Nat Methods.* 2017;14:417-419.
* **Decoy-aware selective alignment** — Srivastava A, et al. Alignment and mapping methodology influence transcript abundance estimation. *Genome Biol.* 2020;21:239.
* **STAR** — Dobin A, et al. STAR: ultrafast universal RNA-seq aligner. *Bioinformatics.* 2013;29:15-21.
* **SAMtools** — Danecek P, et al. Twelve years of SAMtools and BCFtools. *GigaScience.* 2021;10:giab008.
* **gffread** — Pertea G, Pertea M. GFF Utilities: GffRead and GffCompare. *F1000Research.* 2020;9:304.
* **tximport** — Soneson C, Love MI, Robinson MD. Differential analyses for RNA-seq: transcript-level estimates improve gene-level inferences. *F1000Research.* 2015;4:1521.
* **DESeq2** — Love MI, Huber W, Anders S. Moderated estimation of fold change and dispersion for RNA-seq data with DESeq2. *Genome Biol.* 2014;15:550.
* **apeglm** — Zhu A, Ibrahim JG, Love MI. Heavy-tailed prior distributions for sequence count data. *Bioinformatics.* 2019;35:2084-2092.
* **MultiQC** — Ewels P, et al. MultiQC: summarize analysis results for multiple tools and samples in a single report. *Bioinformatics.* 2016;32:3047-3048.
* **Bioconda** — Grüning B, et al. Bioconda: sustainable and comprehensive software distribution for the life sciences. *Nat Methods.* 2018;15:475-476.
